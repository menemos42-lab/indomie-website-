The Golden Rule

The 3-Minute Ritual

Precision architecture is required to construct the perfect strand texture. Follow the blueprint meticulously.

1. The Rolling Boil

Submerge blocks in exactly 400ml of roaring, high-pressure boiling water. Set the countdown for 3 minutes precisely.

2. Seasoning Canvas

While cooking, arrange the structural oils, thick sweet soy, and spicy components directly onto your plate layout. Do not mix prematurely.

3. The Fusion Emulsion

Drain water completely. Toss dry noodles aggressively into the seasoning canvas until every solitary strand possesses a brilliant amber gloss.
